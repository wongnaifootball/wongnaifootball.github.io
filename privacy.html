Privacy Policy — WongNai Football Publisher

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What the App accesses

WongNai Football Publisher ("the App") connects only to the channel owner's own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. With the owner's permission it uses:

  • user.info.basic — the account's display name and avatar, to confirm the correct account is connected;
  • video.upload / video.publish — to upload and publish the channel's own videos.

How data is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never shared or sold. The App does not collect, store or process personal data of TikTok viewers or any other users. No analytics or advertising trackers are used by the App.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Changes and contact

Updates are posted on this page. Contact us via TikTok @wongnaifootball.
