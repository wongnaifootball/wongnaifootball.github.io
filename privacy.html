Privacy Policy — WongNai Football Publisher

Last updated: 13 November 2026

WongNai Football Publisher ("the App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the WongNai Football (วงในฟุตบอล) channel to publish the channel's own videos and posts to the channel's own accounts on Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

What the App accesses

  • Facebook: the WongNai Football Page only (pages_show_list, pages_manage_posts, pages_read_engagement, read_insights) — to publish Reels and photo posts, and to read comments, reactions and view counts on the Page's own posts.
  • TikTok: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish — to confirm the connected account and upload/publish the channel's own videos.
  • YouTube: upload and read-only access to the channel's own videos.

Viewer data

For the "guess the score" fan activity, the App reads public comments on the channel's own posts and shows the display name (first name and last-name initial) of fans who guessed correctly. No other personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared. No advertising or analytics trackers are used by the App.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared.
